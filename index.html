SENTINEL

Your home command centre. Your computer is HQ. Old phones and tablets become Agents.
Nothing to download or install.

▶ START HQOn the computer that stays at home. Keep this tab open. ◉ BE AN AGENTOn an old phone or tablet. (Easier: scan the QR code that HQ shows.) ▤ MY PHONESee HQ from anywhere, after linking it in HQ’s Settings → Phones.

How it works

  1. On your computer, press START HQ. Bookmark it. (Chrome/Edge: ⋮ → Cast, save and share → Install page as app to give it its own window.)
  2. Press ＋ RECRUIT. Scan the QR code with an old phone on your Wi-Fi. It appears on the radar.
  3. Click the Agent and give it a mission: MOTION WATCH, DOOR TRIPWIRE, SOUND LEVEL, LIGHT CHECK, DOOR PANEL or INTEL SCREEN.
  4. Press AWAY when everyone leaves. Movement then means THREAT RED.

Privacy: missions work on the Agent itself — HQ only receives events like “motion detected” or “72 dB”. You can open Live view to see an Agent’s camera; it isn’t recorded or saved, and the Agent shows a red LIVE VIEW banner while you watch. Devices talk to each other directly over an encrypted link; a free matchmaking service only introduces them. Your Mission Log stays in your HQ computer’s browser.
